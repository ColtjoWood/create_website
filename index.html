About Me

I am Colt Wood, a Computer Science student at Texas Tech University , pursuing a Bachelor of Science in Computer Science with a minor in Mathematics. I expect to graduate in Spring 2027. My coursework and hands-on experience have given me a foundation in programming, computer systems, networking, data structures, algorithms, and software development.

I currently work as a Tier 1 IT Specialist at Triumph Public High Schools, where I provide technical support for staff and campus technology. My work includes troubleshooting hardware and software, network connectivity, Ethernet drops, switch ports, PoE devices, IP phones, printers, Windows systems, and other network-connected equipment. This experience has strengthened my interest in networking, IT infrastructure, and hands-on technical problem solving.

Outside of work and coursework, I enjoy building projects that allow me to apply what I am learning. One of my recent projects is a multi-site enterprise network designed in Cisco Packet Tracer. The network incorporates VLAN segmentation, OSPF routing, DHCP, DNS, NAT/PAT, VoIP, Wi-Fi, port security, and LACP redundancy. Building and troubleshooting the network gave me practical experience with both Layer 2 and Layer 3 networking concepts.

My technical background also includes C++, C, Python, Java, Assembly, JavaScript, HTML/CSS, and React.js, along with experience using Windows, Linux, Git/GitHub, and Cisco Packet Tracer. As I continue my degree, I am especially interested in expanding my knowledge of networking, systems, and IT infrastructure and applying those skills to real-world environments!
